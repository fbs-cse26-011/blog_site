Reflecting on My Academic Journey

On independence, study habits and learning to see challenges as a place to grow.

Introduction

Transitioning into tertiary education at SCIS has been a transformative experience. Looking back on my first semester, I have realised that university is not only about acquiring technical knowledge; it is also about developing self-management skills and effective study strategies.

Adapting to Tertiary Education

When I first started, I underestimated the level of independence required. In high school, teachers often guided us step by step. At university, I had to take ownership of my learning. I quickly realised that time management was my biggest hurdle.

To adapt, I began using a digital calendar and the Pomodoro Technique. Breaking my study sessions into focused intervals helped me to avoid procrastination and to manage the heavy workload more effectively. I also started blocking out specific hours in Google Calendar for classes, assignments and personal time, which helped me to meet deadlines while maintaining a healthy balance.

Developing a Growth Mindset

One of the most valuable concepts I have learned is the growth mindset. Initially, when I encountered complex topics in System Development, I felt overwhelmed. However, I learned to view challenges not as failures, but as opportunities to grow.

By seeking help during consultations and using online resources such as freeCodeCamp, I turned my weaknesses into areas of strength. I also learned that asking for help is a sign of commitment to learning, not a sign of weakness.

Looking Ahead

I recognise that building good academic habits is an ongoing process. In the coming semesters, I intend to keep refining my study routine, plan ahead for assessments and continue seeking feedback from my lecturers and peers.

Conclusion

Overall, my academic journey so far has taught me resilience and the importance of proactive study habits. I am learning that success depends not only on intelligence, but also on consistency, asking for help when needed and maintaining a positive attitude toward learning. I look forward to applying these strategies as I advance in my studies.
