Study Tips & Resources

These are some of the strategies and tools that have helped me succeed in my first year.

1. The Pomodoro Technique

  • What it is: Studying in focused 25-minute intervals, followed by a 5-minute break.
  • Why it works for me: It prevents burnout and keeps me focused on one task at a time.

2. Active Recall and Flashcards

  • Tools: Quizlet or physical flashcards.
  • Why it works for me: Instead of simply re-reading my notes, testing myself helps me to remember definitions and concepts for exams much more effectively.

3. Useful YouTube Channels and Websites

  • freeCodeCamp.org – free tutorials on programming and computer science.
  • FastBit Embedded Brain Academy – courses on embedded systems and microcontroller programming.

4. Time Blocking

  • What it is: I use Google Calendar to block out specific hours for classes, assignments and personal time.
  • Why it works for me: It helps me to meet deadlines and maintain a healthy study–life balance.
